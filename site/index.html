Open source

Bring your own model.

The music engine is a small program that speaks JSON lines over stdin and stdout. YuE2 on MLX ships built in. Point Settings at any other model and the rest of the app stays the same: your artists, the player and sharing.

  • Private by design. Songs are recorded on your Mac and stay in a SQLite library you own.
  • Your ChatGPT subscription writes. Artists, albums and lyrics come from GPT‑6.1 Terra, signed in with your own account.
  • Share anything. A .standardalbum file carries the artist, their soul and the recordings. AirDrop it to a friend.
